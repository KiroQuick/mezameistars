## 2. Business Summary

The business is a handmade wood-and-epoxy product brand. The main products are:

1. **Epoxy chess boards**  
   Handmade chess boards created from natural wood and epoxy resin. These can be used for playing chess, as gifts, or as decorative display pieces.

2. **Pasniegšanas dēļi / serving boards**  
   Handmade serving boards made from wood and epoxy, intended for food presentation, home decor, kitchens, gifts, weddings, and special occasions.

The website should present the business as a premium handmade craft brand focused on unique, useful, and visually attractive wooden products.

## 11. Color Palette

The client provided the following color palette:

| Color Name | Hex Code | Suggested Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Jet Black | `#1B2F33` | Main background, header, footer, luxury sections |
| Dark Spruce | `#28502E` | Secondary background, natural sections, hover states |
| Yellow Green | `#84C318` | Buttons, highlights, small accents |
| Olive Wood | `#8C7051` | Wood accent, borders, product tags, warm details |
| Dust Grey | `#E2DADB` | Light backgrounds, cards, text contrast |

### Color usage recommendation

Use `#1B2F33` as the main dark background. Use `#E2DADB` for readable light sections and product cards. Use `#8C7051` to bring in a warm wood feeling. Use `#84C318` only for important buttons and small highlights, because it is very bright.
